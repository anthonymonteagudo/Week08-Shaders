Week 8 · Activities

Use the gallery setup first. Canvas provides deadlines and submission requirements.

Lab 1 · Calm cove and exposed water

20 minutes: 3 briefing, 15 investigation, 2 comparison. Sheltered water is protected from wind; exposed water is open to it. Make gentler and stronger motion as artistic targets.

  1. In gallery bay 03, choose a row and duplicate its final MI twice. Assign your copies to displays. Keep camera, lighting, mesh, colors, and finish fixed.
  2. Test one control at three values. Record the values, prediction, and result.
  3. Use at most two controls to make sheltered and exposed variants. Show a classmate without naming them; ask what motion distinguishes them.
  4. Save both MIs and two captures or a short clip. Write three sentences: what changed, which input/operation it affects, and what you observed. Use three relevant shader terms.

Browser route when Unreal is unavailable

Open lesson 03. Keep Tiling 2 and both colors fixed. Test Speed 0, 0.12, −0.12; then compare 0.04 and 0.16 for gentler/stronger motion. Save the values and captures. Negative Speed reverses direction; it is not wind strength. This route moves a texture on a flat plane; it does not deform geometry.

Lab 2 · One original shader idea

25 minutes: 3 briefing, 22 independent work. Begin the homework with one small visual goal, such as a pulse, stripe, reveal, or mask combination.

  1. Keep baseline A; work in duplicate B. Add or change one operation or behavior and expose a useful parameter. Nodes are enough.
  2. Capture the result and a readable graph/code view.
  3. Write about five sentences: goal, inputs, calculation, observation, next step. Use three class terms and credit any adapted source.

Save A/B, camera, resolution, and settings for Week 9. A visible change does not prove a speed improvement; performance measurement comes next week.
