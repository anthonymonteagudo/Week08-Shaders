Key sources for materials and shading

Use the source pointers beside each slide to follow up on a specific idea. The notes below identify a useful starting section and how the source relates to our Unreal examples.

Ned’s final slide credits LearnOpenGL.com as inspiration for his diagrams. Other entries are course reading suggestions. Engine manuals support specific controls; tutorials and production talks provide broader study. A named game reference does not imply that our classroom material reproduces its renderer.
