    How to use the comparison
    Translate the calculation, not the entire engine wrapper. vec3 becomes float3; mix becomes lerp; clamp(x, 0, 1) can become saturate(x). Texture sampling needs the engine's texture and sampler inputs. Keep vector spaces and data color-space settings consistent.
    In the Unreal gallery, follow the numbered bays. Use your own duplicate instances to experiment. The web renderer and Unreal may differ in exposure, color management and geometry; compare operations and behavior rather than identical RGB values.
